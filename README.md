## 👋 About the Workshop

**The Trans Girl Workshop** is an independent development studio working on games, Discord bots, mods, utilities, and experimental projects.

Projects range from horror games and community tools to game mods and quality-of-life utilities, with a focus on building things that are useful, interesting, atmospheric, or just a little unusual.

---

## 🎮 Current Game Project

### 👁️ OBSCURA INDEX — VAULT ZERO

A desk-bound psychological horror and anomaly investigation game currently in development.

Review photographic and audio evidence, assemble procedural cases, report anomalies, track increasingly disturbing patterns, and survive the growing awareness that something may be watching you back.

**OBSCURA INDEX — VAULT ZERO** features:

- 📸 Photo and audio evidence review
- 🗂️ Procedural case assembly
- 👁️ Anomaly recognition and reporting
- ☎️ Phone and intercom events
- 🧠 Player behaviour tracking
- 📖 Procedural story chains
- 🏢 Escalating office events
- 🔀 Multiple endings
- 💾 Previous-run persistence
